Both files are the latin subset only (~56 KB combined). Characters outside
that range fall back to the next family in the stack, which is the intended
behavior — see the unicode-range declarations in src/styles/global.css.

Why self-hosted rather than loaded from Google Fonts: no third-party request
on page load, and no dependency on an external host remaining available.

To replace either typeface, drop in a new .woff2, update the matching
@font-face block and the --font-display / --font-body tokens in
src/styles/global.css, and check --fw-display still matches an available
weight. Nothing else references the font files.
